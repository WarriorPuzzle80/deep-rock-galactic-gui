**Deep Rock Galactic GUI** is a comprehensive overlay for Deep Rock Galactic featuring tactical minimap, loot tracker, resource radar, enemy ESP, configurable HUD, and more.

## ✨ Features

### 🗺️ Tactical Minimap
- Full cave minimap with real-time player and enemy positions
- Resource nodes and loot highlighted
- Customizable zoom and opacity

### 💰 Loot & Resource Tracker
- Track all minerals, gold, and secondary objectives
- Notifications for rare loot and events
- Distance indicators to nearest resources

### 👁️ Enemy ESP
- See enemies through walls with health bars
- Highlight dangerous creatures like Glyphids and Mactera
- Customizable colors and visibility ranges

### 🎮 HUD Customization
- Adjustable HUD scale and position
- Toggle individual HUD elements
- Custom crosshair and hit markers
